## In one paragraph

The C++ solver (`bin/param`, Haro–Luque parameterization method adapted to the Saturn–Enceladus
CR3BP) converges quadratically and computes Lagrangian invariant 2-tori of the Poincaré map on
q3 = 0 to a sup-norm invariance error of ~4e-13 LU (~0.1 mm) for tori a few km across, and below
1e-11 LU (~2 mm) for every accepted torus. Five families (454 tori) around five stable NRHOs are
computed and stored in `results/families/`, with figures in `figures/`. A 19-page paper draft is
in `docs/paper/`. There are **no known open bugs**; the open items are listed at the end.

## Why the families do not start at the NRHO

The families are not continued *from* the NRHO. Each one starts from a torus of amplitude
4×10⁻⁵ LU along the two elliptic eigendirections (radius ~5 km on the section), fitted to an
actual orbit (`family_pipeline.jl`), and is then continued in both directions:

* **inward**, toward the NRHO (ε → −1 is the NRHO itself, where the torus shrinks to a point);
* **outward**, to larger tori.

The inward branches stop before reaching the NRHO: Id 97 at 0.67 km, Id 85 at 1.04 km, Id 53 at
1.35 km, and — earlier than they could — Id 23 at 2.72 km and Id 37 at 4.11 km. That is the gap
between the NRHO marker and the family in the frequency and size plots (largest for Ids 23 and 37).
The method needs a torus of nonzero size: as the torus shrinks, its tangent vectors DK → 0, the
twist (frequency change per unit action) goes to zero and the error measured relative to the
torus size grows, so the steps get small and eventually the continuation stops. The last ~1 km
is expected to stay out of reach; the inward branches of Ids 23 and 37 could be restarted to get
closer. Close to the NRHO the tori are well described by the linearization anyway.

## How to reproduce

From the repository root (`REPO=$(pwd)`):

```bash
make                                                        # builds bin/param (C++, OpenMP, -O2)
julia --project=. src/julia/family_pipeline.jl 53 /tmp/fam53   # fixed point, start torus, setup.txt
# continuation: one branch per directory (param writes output_torus<eps> into the current directory);
# set the step (column 11 of the first line of start.csv) to +0.01 (outward) or -0.01 (inward)
cd /tmp/fam53 && OMP_NUM_THREADS=12 $REPO/bin/param start.csv --domega <d1> <d2> \
     --eps-max 100 --max-steps 3000 --tol-floor 1e-11      # <d1> <d2>: the "domega" line of setup.txt
cd $REPO
julia --project=. src/julia/family_analysis.jl figures/family_id53/id53 inward=<dir> outward=<dir>
NRHO_ID=53 julia --project=. src/julia/qpo_visualization.jl figures/qpo3d/id53 30 <torus files>
```

`bin/param` modes for checks: `--test-twist 2` (integrable twist map, quadratic convergence to
1e-16), `--fdcheck` (Jacobian vs finite differences), `--orbit`, `--eval`.
